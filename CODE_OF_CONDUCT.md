# Code of Conduct

f6 is a project about shared meaning, so we aim to be a place where people can disagree about meaning without disrespect.

**We expect everyone to:**

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people working in a second language.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Credit others' work and respect the cultural origins of what we build on.

**We do not tolerate:**

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Deliberately disruptive or bad-faith behavior.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may remove people from the project, when these expectations are not met. To report a problem, open an issue marked *conduct*, or contact a maintainer privately (add a contact address here before publishing).

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests and discussions.
