# The Ideological Money Pump (IMP)

*Money earned from SophiOS flows back to those who have less.*

The Ideological Money Pump is part of the project license, the [Physix Perpetualicense and Ideological Money Pump](../LICENSE). Read the license for the exact terms; this page explains the idea.

## In simple words

You are free to use, change and share everything in f6, and you may even sell things you build with it. There is one promise that comes with SophiOS:

> **If you make a profit from SophiOS, at least 10% of that profit must be given to people who are less fortunate.**

Think of a pump: money comes in from the work, and a part of it is pushed back out to people who need it. If you earn nothing, you owe nothing.

## The rule

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| What is covered? | SophiOS, and anything derived from it |
| What do I owe? | At least **10%** of **Profit** |
| What is Profit? | Revenue from SophiOS, minus the reasonable, documented direct costs of earning it |
| Who receives it? | People in need of material support, directly or through a charity that serves them |
| How often? | At least once per calendar year in which you earned Profit |
| What if I earn nothing? | You owe nothing |
| Can I give to myself or my company? | No. Gifts to you or to anyone you control do not count |
| Does it pass on to others? | Yes. Anyone you give SophiOS to receives it under the same license |
| Can I give more? | Please do |

## Open questions

These need decisions; the license text currently picks the simplest reading of each.

- **What counts as Profit?** The draft says revenue minus reasonable direct costs. Should it be net income instead, or a share of revenue?
- **What counts as "derived"?** How much of SophiOS must a product contain before the rule applies?
- **Who counts as "less fortunate"?** The draft says people in need of material support. Is a stricter definition or an approved list needed?
- **Enforcement.** Who checks the records, and what happens on non-payment?
- **Other parts of f6.** The IMP covers SophiOS. Should Physix, the Svensk Army Knife and the lexicon be covered too?
- **Currency and tax.** How are gifts valued across countries and currencies?
- **"Perpetual".** The license name says *Perpetualicense*, but the text does not yet say that the grant is perpetual. Decide whether to add that.
