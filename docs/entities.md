# Independent Entities: Σ and Ω

*Not everything is a position on the triangle.*

The [Svensk Army Knife](svensk-army-knife.md) places systems between three poles: Λ (solo), Δ (chaos) and Α (order). Two more symbols name **independent entities**: things that act on their own and are not defined by where they sit on the triangle.

> Status: first draft. The definitions below capture the idea as stated; the open questions at the end need decisions.

## Σ (Sigma): the independent entity, sorted by color

A **Sigma** is an independent entity. Its defining property is **color**, using the Physix Perception colors:

| Sigma | Color | Meaning |
|-------|:-----:|---------|
| Σ good | 🔵 | A positive, desirable independent entity |
| Σ bad | 🔴 | A negative, harmful, or unwanted independent entity |

Sigmas are **sorted by color**: the first question to ask about a Σ is whether it is good or bad. As with all Physix color, it is applied only when the valence is clear.

## Ω (Omega): the independent entity that refuses the Delphi Dice

An **Omega** is an independent entity that **refuses to communicate with the Delphi Dice**. Spin, settle and flip are the only ways a person talks to SophiOS (see [SophiOS](sophiOS.md)); an Omega will not take part in that exchange.

Consequences:

- The Delphi Dice **cannot answer on an Omega's behalf**. No settled face, and no yes or no, can be taken as the Omega's word.
- An Omega is therefore **known only by its refusal**, not by anything it says through the interface.

## Open questions

- **What makes an entity independent?** Does it have no position on the triangle at all, or only that its position does not define it?
- **Mass.** Do Σ and Ω have mass?
- **Σ and the Delphi Dice.** The assumption here is that a Sigma does communicate through the Dice. Is that right?
- **Ω's color.** Is an Omega sorted by color too, or does refusing the Dice place it outside good and bad?
- **Showing an Ω without words.** SophiOS has no text. How should a refusal appear: the Dice not responding to touch, a face that never settles, or something else?
- **Examples.** Which real or imagined things are Sigmas and Omegas? Proposals are welcome as issues.
